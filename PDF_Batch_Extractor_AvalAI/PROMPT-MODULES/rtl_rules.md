# Right-to-Left (RTL) Text Rules

## Detection
- Persian (Farsi) and Arabic text flows RIGHT-TO-LEFT
- English and Latin text flows LEFT-TO-RIGHT
- Mixed text: Each segment follows its own direction

## Rules
1. **Reading order**: For RTL text, read from the RIGHT side of the page to the LEFT
2. **Mixed lines**: When a line contains both Persian and English, maintain the natural bidirectional flow as it appears
3. **Parenthetical references**: Medical/academic references in English within Persian text (e.g., `(Diagnostic)`) should stay in their original position
4. **Numbers in RTL context**: Persian numbers (۱۲۳) and Arabic-Indic numbers stay as-is. Latin numbers (123) within Persian text also stay as-is
5. **Line alignment**: Do not reverse the order of words within a line — preserve the visual reading order
